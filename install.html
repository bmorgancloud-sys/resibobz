iPhone steps

⚠️ First: open this page in Safari You opened this inside another app (like WhatsApp). Tap the Safari button or the ••• button, then tap Open in Safari. Then come back to step 1.

  1. Open Safari.

    Safari is the blue compass on your phone.

    Safari

  2. Go to the app.

    Tap this button. It opens Toucan Bill.

    Open Toucan Bill

  3. Tap the Share button.

    It is a square with an arrow going up. It is at the bottom of the screen. (On some iPhones, tap ••• first.)

    Share

  4. Tap “Add to Home Screen”.

    You may need to slide the list up to find it.

    Add to Home Screen

  5. Tap “Add”.

    It is at the top right. If you see “Open as Web App”, leave it turned on.

    Add

    Look on your home screen. Tap the toucan icon to open Toucan Bill.

    Toucan Bill

Computer steps

Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.

  1. Open Chrome or Edge.

    Then go to toucanbill.netlify.app or click this button.

    Open Toucan Bill

  2. Find the install button.

    Look at the top, on the right side of the web address. It looks like a little screen with a down arrow.

    toucanbill.netlify.app

    Can’t see it? Click the ⋮ or ••• menu, then Install Toucan Bill (in Edge: Apps → Install this site as an app).

  3. Click “Install”.

    Toucan Bill opens in its own window.

  4. You did it! 🎉

    Next time, open it from your desktop, Start menu or Dock.

✏️ First time you open it

  1. Tap Settings (the gear).

    Type your business name, address, phone and GST TIN. Add your logo if you like.

  2. Tap “Save settings”.

    Now your name shows on every receipt.

  3. Tap “New” to make a receipt.

    Add the customer and items, then tap Save receipt. Share it on WhatsApp.

🔒 Keep your receipts safe

  • 🏠
    Open it from the home screen icon. Not from Safari or Chrome.
  • 📱
    Your receipts live on this phone. If you delete the app or clear your browser, they are gone.
  • 💾
    Make a backup every week. Settings → Export backup. Send the file to yourself on WhatsApp or save it to iCloud / Google Drive.
  • 🔄
    See “Update available”? Tap it. Your receipts stay safe.
  • ❌
    Made a mistake? Void the receipt. Don’t delete it. This keeps your GST right.
